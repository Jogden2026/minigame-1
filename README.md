## Devlog
If I had to explain the relationship of Components, GameObjects, and Scenes, I would probably use a recent lesson I had in biology since it feels so applicable, specifically proteins. Proteins have a TON of uses in our body, like being enzymes and carrying out all the orders of DNA. As programmers, we are the DNA that determines what proteins are made and how they are constructed. At the smallest level of a protein are amino acids, which are small compounds which all start the same, but are defined by their R groups. In the GameDev scene, the R groups are the Components, because they determine how amino acids are going to behave. Amino Acids are the GameObjects, as without R groups (Components), they serve little function. When you line up a bunch of amino acids (GameObjects), you get a Scene, which is the protein. Scenes are proteins because they can either serve an individual purpose (like being instructions), or can be added to other proteins (Scenes) to have more complex purposes (like becoming enzymes or in GameDev multiple scenes in a game). This metaphor is sort of convoluted if the person doesn't know cell biology pretty well, but there are so many parallels it feels almost too right not to compare. In really basic terms, the Components are the flavor of the GameObjects, and a mix of GameObjects is what makes a Scene functional.
